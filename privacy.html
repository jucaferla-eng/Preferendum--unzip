Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Preferendum ("we", "our", or "the platform") is committed to protecting the privacy of all users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Preferendum mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

To create and verify your account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • National identity document number
  • Gender and date of birth
  • Country, city and district of residence
  • Device identifier (IMEI)
  • Approximate geolocation

2. Identity Verification (8 Layers)

Preferendum uses an 8-layer identity verification system to ensure that every voter is a real, unique person:

  • Email OTP
  • SMS verification
  • Identity document
  • Selfie with document
  • Device verification (IMEI)
  • Geolocation
  • Blockchain Polygon anchoring
  • Phone number and SIM card verification

3. How Your Vote is Protected

Your vote is protected by a process called bridge destruction:

  • Your vote is encrypted with AES-256-CBC before being stored
  • A SHA-256 hash of your vote is anchored on the Polygon blockchain
  • After your vote is recorded, your voter ID is permanently unlinked from your vote
  • You receive a unique XXXX-XXXX-XXXX verification code to confirm your vote was counted correctly
  • No one — including Preferendum — can link your identity to your specific vote

4. Demographic Data

After bridge destruction, we retain anonymous demographic records (gender, age group, district, country) for statistical purposes only. These records are never linked to your identity or your vote.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity and create your account
  • To determine which consultations you are eligible to participate in
  • To send you notifications about consultations in your area
  • To generate anonymous statistical reports on participation
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse

6. Advertising

Preferendum displays sponsored ads between opinions in the debate room. These ads are targeted using anonymous demographic data (income proxy by district, age group, gender and consultation category). We never share your personal identity with advertisers.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share anonymized, aggregated data for research or statistical reporting. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at privacy@preferendum.com.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

10. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS. Votes are encrypted with AES-256-CBC. Identity is anchored on Polygon blockchain.

11. Children

Preferendum is available to users aged 13 and older. Users under 18 may only participate in consultations appropriate for their age group.
