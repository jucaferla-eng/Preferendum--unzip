How it works

Four steps to
verified answers.

1
You ask
Create your consultation. Define the question, the options, and who can participate. Local, national, or international.
2
They debate
Participants read the context and discuss. Opinions organized by knowledge level — expert voices and newcomers coexist.
3
They vote
Anonymous, encrypted vote. Each voter receives a unique code. Results visible in real time.
4
They verify
Voters confirm their vote was counted correctly. The Legitimacy Score is born. Anchored in blockchain forever.
